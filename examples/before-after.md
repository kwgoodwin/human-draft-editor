# Editing examples

All examples below are synthetic. The before-text contains every factual premise used in the after-text. These are illustrations, not guaranteed model outputs.

## Clearer legal prose without a new holding

**Before**

It is important to note that on November 6 the court granted permission to file the brief. That distinction matters. The order did not decide whether training is fair use.

**After**

On November 6, the court allowed the brief to be filed. The order did not decide whether training is fair use.

**Why:** removes framing, preserves the date and procedural limit, and does not turn acceptance of a filing into acceptance of its argument.

## Business writing with the condition intact

**Before**

At this point in time, we expect the pilot to begin on May 12, provided that the security review is complete. The pilot may reduce processing time; the results are not yet known.

**After**

We expect the pilot to begin on May 12 if the security review is complete. It may reduce processing time; the results are not yet known.

**Why:** shortens the prose without making the start date unconditional or promising a benefit.

## A voice sample is not a biography to borrow

**Voice sample:** “I like short meetings. Give me the decision and the reason.”

**Before**

Our team tested two ways to organize the project notes. The shorter format was easier for three reviewers to scan, but we have not tested it with the whole team.

**After**

Our team tested two ways to organize the project notes. Three reviewers found the shorter format easier to scan. We have not tested it with the whole team.

**Why:** uses direct sentences without inventing personal feelings, a rollout decision or wider evidence.

## No change is a useful result

**Before and after, unchanged**

The cache expires after 15 minutes. A failed refresh does not extend that interval. Keep serving the previous value until the fallback timer expires.

**Why:** already clear. Adding conversational filler or swapping technical terms would not improve it.

## Do not solve a factual problem with nicer prose

**Before**

The trial proves the treatment is safe for everyone.

**Style-only disposition**

Preserve the claim and flag its universal scope for factual review when notes are allowed. Do not replace it with “The trial suggests the treatment may be safe” without authorization and evidence: that is a substantive change, not a style repair.
